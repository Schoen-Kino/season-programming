Season 9: Recordações da Casa Amarela

Some call it a masterpiece, others say it’s the worst they’ve ever seen. João de Deus (João of God) is everything but a holy man. As a matter of fact, he is utterly disgusting: a perverted intellectual, passionate about Schubert, cinema, and… the landlord’s daughter. This can only go downhill from here. Like last month’s Timothy Treadwell, João is an outcast from society. The film follows him, witness to a series of absurd encounters in a Lisbon that no longer exists. A comedy of the absurd, a really boring movie, or simply bad? I guess we’ll find out. In true Lisbon spirit, better watched (or rather washed down?) with beer.
